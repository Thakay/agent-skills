# Choosing personas

Pick the 3 to 5 people who would actually read this document for this target, and who decide something because of it. Cover these roles when they exist:

- **The decider:** the person whose yes moves the author forward, such as the hiring manager, the partner or the maintainer.
- **The expert peer:** someone who does the work the document describes and spots inflated or vague claims.
- **The skeptic or gatekeeper:** a security reviewer, a compliance lead, a senior researcher or anyone paid to find the weak point.
- **The fast screener:** someone who reads hundreds of these and gives each about a minute.
- **The end user,** when the document sells something to them.

Write each persona in two or three sentences: who they are, what they weigh, and what a yes means to them. Make them specific to the target; "a senior engineer" is weaker than "a research engineer who builds RL environments for coding agents". Do not give a persona the author's private context or opinions.

Choose fit axes from what the target itself asks for, such as the two sides of a combined role, or "technical depth" and "clarity".

## Common document types

| Document and target | Personas | Decision | Axes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resume for a job | hiring manager, peer engineer on the team, recruiter screener, skip-level leader | advance to the first interview | role fit, seniority, credibility |
| Resume for a research fellowship or grant | mentor in the research area, research scientist, program screener, systems or infrastructure peer | advance to the technical assessment | research potential, engineering, area fit |
| Pitch deck | partner at a fund that invests at this stage, analyst, a founder in the space, a skeptical domain expert | take the meeting | market, team, traction |
| README or open-source project | a developer evaluating it for work, a maintainer of a similar project, a security reviewer, a first-time user | star and try it | usefulness, trust, ease of first use |
| Blog post or essay | an expert in the topic, a newcomer, an editor, a skeptic | read to the end and share it | clarity, originality, accuracy |
| Design doc or RFC | a senior engineer who will review it, the on-call owner, a product lead, a security reviewer | approve as written | soundness, risk, completeness |
| Landing page | a buyer in the target segment, a technical evaluator, a competitor's marketer, a first-time visitor | sign up or book a demo | value clarity, trust, call to action |

These rows are starting points. Replace any persona the target makes irrelevant, and add one the target names, such as a review board described in a call for proposals.
